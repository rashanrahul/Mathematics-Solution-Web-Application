# 5. Step-by-Step Solution

මේක website එකේ **ප්‍රධාන feature එක**.

උදාහරණයක්:

**Question:**

```text
2x + 5 = 15
```

**Solution:**

### Step 1

Subtract 5 from both sides.

```text
2x + 5 - 5 = 15 - 5
```

### Step 2

```text
2x = 10
```

### Step 3

Divide both sides by 2.

```text
x = 10 / 2
```

### Final Answer

```text
x = 5
```

ඒ වගේ **studentට තේරෙන විදිහට** solution එක explain කරන්න ඕන.

# 6. Explanation Mode

Userට solution level එක select කරන්න දෙන්න.

### 🟢 Simple

Beginner/student friendly explanation.

### 🔵 Standard

Normal mathematical explanation.

### 🟣 Detailed

Formula + reasoning + complete derivation.

උදාහරණයක්:

> **Why do we subtract 5?**
> We subtract 5 to remove the constant term from the left side and isolate `2x`.

මේ feature එක learning purpose එකට වැදගත්.

# 7. Formula Display

Solution එකේදී භාවිතා කරන formula එක පෙන්වන්න.

උදාහරණයක්:

**Circle Area**

```text
A = πr²
```

Given:

```text
r = 7
```

Therefore:

```text
A = π × 7²
A = 49π
A ≈ 153.94 cm²
```

# 8. Answer Verification


